# Primerjava

Calculator for door-to-door fibre sales: what the customer pays now vs. the T-2 offer, the monthly and 2-year saving, the net saving after the cancellation fee, and a 2-page A4 sheet to share as a PDF.

Installable on Android (Chrome → Add to home screen) and works offline. Prices are editable in the app and stored on the device. Unofficial tool, not a T-2 document.
